# Assessment: What an Assistant Can Reach

## Easy

### 1. In simple words, what is the difference between an answer that fits and an answer that is true?
* **An answer that fits** matches the requested topic, length, and tone (it sounds complete and right).
* **An answer that is true** is accurate in the real world.
* An assistant naturally produces answers that fit, but only a human check can verify if an answer is true.

### 2. Name the three kinds of AI help taught in this lesson.
1. **Chat** (Words in, words out)
2. **Live search** (Reaching outside for real-time information)
3. **Voice** (Changing the channel / audio input and output)

### 3. What is a human check?
A **human check** is the step where a person verifies an AI’s answer against an official or reliable source before trusting or using it, rather than assuming it is accurate just because it sounds confident.

## Intermediate

### 1. A college fest team wants today's ticket price before printing a poster. Should they use normal chat, live search, or voice? Explain why.
* **Choice:** Live search
* **Explanation:** Ticket prices change and are date-sensitive. Normal chat relies on old memory/training data, whereas live search reaches out to retrieve current, real-time information.

### 2. An AI gives a confident film fact for a public poster. What should the team do before using it?
* **What they should do:** Perform a human check.
* **Details:** The team must open an official source (such as the film's official database or webpage) to compare and confirm the fact before putting it on a public poster, because confidence in tone does not guarantee factual accuracy.

## Hard

### 1. For each task below, choose chat, live search, or voice. Give one reason for each choice.

* **Write a short caption from details you already know:**
  * **Tool:** Chat
  * **Reason:** It is a basic "words in, words out" task that only requires text formatting/summarizing from provided text, not live web data.

* **Find today's train cancellation update:**
  * **Tool:** Live search
  * **Reason:** This requires real-time, up-to-date live information from external official sources.

* **Hear a reminder while your hands are busy:**
  * **Tool:** Voice
  * **Reason:** Voice changes the output channel to audio so you can receive the information hands-free when you cannot read off a screen.

### 2. Write one situation where an AI answer needs a human check and one situation where a quick private answer needs less checking. Explain the difference.

* **Situation needing a strong human check:** Posting a "confirmed" exam date or a fest detail in a class WhatsApp group or on a public poster.
* **Situation needing less checking:** Getting a one-line summary of a missed WhatsApp group chat or generating creative study group names just for yourself.
* **Explanation of the difference:** The level of checking depends on where the information is going and who will see it. When claims are public and other people will see and rely on them, they carry real consequences and need an official check. When information is private and low-stakes, a quick personal skim is sufficient.
